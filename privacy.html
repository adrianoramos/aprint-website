Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how the Aprint application for Windows and the aprint.site website handle information. The application captures and edits images; the website presents the project and provides download links.

The Aprint application

Screenshots, annotations, masks, document generation, and text recognition using Windows OCR are processed locally on your computer. Aprint does not send captured images or recognized text to external services.

Depending on your chosen actions and settings, you can copy images to the clipboard, save them to a folder, or add them to a document. Files stay in the locations you choose. An interrupted session may leave a temporary working .docx file beside the configured destination; you can remove it if you do not plan to resume the session. Application preferences are used locally.

Automatic masking only suggests text regions and may not identify all sensitive information. Review the suggestions and apply additional masks before sharing an image. Temporarily revealing the original image may also display its contents on screen.

The aprint.site website

The homepage uses your browser's local storage to remember the selected language. This preference stays in your browser and can be removed in the site's data settings.

The homepage includes Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity scripts. Tag Manager manages the loading of tags; Clarity may collect information about site usage, such as pages visited, interactions, and technical browser or device data. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their settings and policies. Data collected by these services does not include images or text processed by the Aprint application.

The homepage also queries GitHub's public API to display the latest version information. When you visit that page, your browser sends a request to GitHub. Download and source-code links lead to GitHub services, which handle data under their own policies.

Third-party services

Data handling by external services is subject to their respective providers' policies. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

You can control or delete cookies and local data in your browser settings. Blocking third-party scripts may also limit some site features or metrics.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application, website, or services in use change. The date at the top of the page indicates the latest revision.

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the Aprint repository on GitHub. Avoid posting personal information in public requests or comments.
